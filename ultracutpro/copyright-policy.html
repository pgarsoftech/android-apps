Copyright & DMCA Policy

UltraCut Pro respects intellectual property rights.

1. Our Policy

WE DO NOT:

  • Store users' audio files on our servers
  • Enable or facilitate mass copyright infringement
  • Provide copyrighted music for download
  • Have a music library of copyrighted content

WE DO:

  • Provide tools to edit audio files the user already has
  • Respond promptly to valid DMCA takedown notices
  • Terminate accounts of repeat infringers (if applicable)

2. DMCA Takedown Notice Process

If you believe content infringes your copyright, send a notice to: dmca@universalcodestudio.com

Include the following:

  1. Your full name and contact information
  2. Description of the copyrighted work
  3. Description of the infringing material
  4. Your statement of good faith belief
  5. Your statement of accuracy under penalty of perjury
  6. Your electronic or physical signature

We will respond within 48 hours.

3. Counter-Notice Process

If you believe content was wrongly removed, send a counter-notice to: dmca@universalcodestudio.com

Include:

  1. Your identification
  2. Description of removed material
  3. Your statement under penalty of perjury that removal was a mistake
  4. Consent to jurisdiction
  5. Your signature
