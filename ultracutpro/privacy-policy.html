Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 21/05/2026

1. Data We COLLECT

1.1 Audio Files

  • We access audio files ONLY when you select them.
  • Files are processed LOCALLY on your device.
  • We do NOT upload audio files to any server.
  • We do NOT store audio content on our servers.

1.2 Device Permissions Used

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: To read audio files from storage.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: To save exported audio files.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Only when you use the Voice Recorder.
  • READ_CONTACTS: Only when you assign a ringtone to a contact.
  • WRITE_SETTINGS: Only to set the system ringtone or alarm.

1.3 Analytics Data

If utilizing analytics (e.g., Firebase), we may collect anonymous data including:

  • App crash reports
  • Feature usage statistics
  • Device type, OS version, App version
  • Country/Region (not precise location)
  • Session duration

We do NOT collect: Name, Email, Phone, or precise location.

1.4 Purchase Data

  • Managed entirely by Google Play.
  • We only receive a purchase token, product ID, and status.
  • We do NOT store credit card or payment information.

1.5 Local Storage on Device

  • App settings/preferences
  • Project files (saved editing sessions)
  • Export history
  • EQ presets

All stored ONLY on your device — NOT on our servers.

2. Data We DO NOT COLLECT

We explicitly state we DO NOT collect:

  • Real name or personal identity
  • Email address or Phone number
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contents of audio files (we don't read or upload audio data)
  • Contact names or phone numbers (we only use READ_CONTACTS to display the picker)
  • Browsing history, Biometric data, or Financial information

3. How We USE Data

  • Analytics data: To improve app performance and fix bugs.
  • Crash reports: To identify and fix crashes.
  • Purchase data: To verify Pro subscription status.
  • Permissions: Only for the specific feature that needs them.

4. Third Party Services

We may use third-party services that have their own Privacy Policies:

  • Google Play Billing: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. User Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, DPDP Act and other laws, you have rights to access, delete, correct, and port your data. To exercise these rights:

  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data.
  • Clearing app data removes preferences and history.
  • Contact us for specific inquiries.
  • Opt out of analytics via Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately. Parents/guardians who believe their child has provided personal information should contact us.
