Quest of the Archer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play Quest of the Archer (“the Game”). By using the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

The Game may process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers used by Google AdMob to deliver and measure advertisements.
  • Local game data, including progress, scores, unlocked items, settings, and virtual coin balances. This data is stored on your device.
  • Purchase status supplied by Google Play so purchases can be delivered or restored. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Temporary multiplayer session information, such as player inputs and health values, exchanged over the local network during a 1v1 match.
  • Camera access only when you choose to scan a multiplayer QR code. Images are used on-device to read the QR code and are not uploaded by the Game.

We do not require an account and do not knowingly collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the Game, preserve local progress, deliver purchases, provide multiplayer matches, serve and measure advertisements, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game uses:

  • Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Google may process device identifiers and ad interaction data.
  • Google Play Billing to process in-app purchases. Payment information is handled by Google Play, not by us.

These providers process data under their own policies.

5. Advertising & Your Choices

The free version displays banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements from Google AdMob. Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), Google’s User Messaging Platform may show a consent form before ads are requested. Elsewhere, ads load normally without a custom consent popup.

You can open Settings → Ad Privacy for Google privacy options when available, or change ad personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Purchasing Remove Ads or the Premium Bundle removes banner and interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads may still be available if you choose to watch them for coins.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact support@universalcodestudio.com so it can be reviewed and deleted where applicable.

7. EEA and UK Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, port, or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Email support@universalcodestudio.com to make a request. We aim to respond within 30 days, subject to applicable law.

8. California Privacy Rights

California residents may have rights to know, correct, or delete personal information and to opt out of certain sharing for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information. Use Android Settings → Google → Ads, Settings → Ad Privacy when available, or contact support@universalcodestudio.com.

9. Data Security

Google services use encrypted network connections. Game save data is stored locally on your device. No system is completely secure, but we use reasonable measures and limit the data the Game handles.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Local save and preference data remains until you reset progress, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the Game. Temporary multiplayer data is not retained by the Game after the session. Google retains advertising and purchase records according to its own policies and legal obligations. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the Game or applicable law changes. The revised date will appear at the top. Material changes may also be presented in the Game.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, email support@universalcodestudio.com.
